## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Beetleball**. This trainer provides configurable movement and ability assistance for Beetleball matches, including speed control, cooldown management, and defensive protection. It is intended for practice, testing, and private custom lobbies.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to practice beetle classes, test movement and ability interactions, experiment with custom lobbies, or streamline controlled gameplay sessions.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Enable the movement modifier before entering a practice session. Adjust the speed gradually because Beetleball uses momentum-based movement, and excessive values can make precise ball control and landings difficult. Enhanced air control is useful when gliding between elevated map sections.

### Tools & Inventory

Beetleball does not use a traditional item inventory. Progression focuses on beetle classes, player levels, beetle bucks, eggs, hats, skins, trails, stickers, emotes, and other cosmetic rewards. The trainer does not modify cosmetic ownership or platform account data.

### World & Environment

Matches take place on varied maps featuring surfaces and launch points such as dandelions, cakes, donuts, and sandcastles. Use movement options to study routes, momentum transfers, and defensive positions in custom sessions.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is built around class abilities, collisions, knockbacks, and disrupting opposing beetles while teams compete for dungballs. Cooldown control helps compare ability timing and class combinations. Defensive protection should remain limited to private testing.
